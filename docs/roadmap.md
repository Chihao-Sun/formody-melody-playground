# Formody Roadmap

**A staged path from verified prototype to a durable interactive music work.**

**从已核验原型走向长期互动音乐作品的分阶段路线。**

## Now — Verified Baseline

- One regular-pentagon Euclidean Outer Billiards study.
- One movable Seed with pointer, touch, and keyboard input.
- One 16-step monophonic phrase mapped from tangent vertices.
- Browser-synthesized sound with an audio-clock scheduler.
- Explicit mathematical limits and prototype evidence.

## 现在——已核验基线

- 一个正五边形欧几里得 Outer Billiards 研究。
- 一个支持指针、触控和键盘输入的可移动 Seed。
- 一个由切点顶点映射而来的 16 步单声部乐句。
- 一个使用音频时钟调度的浏览器合成声音系统。
- 明确的数学边界与原型证据。

## Next — Musical and Interaction Study

Before implementing, choose the musical language and document the rule:

- melody, bass, rhythm, and controlled variation as explicit layers;
- a shared mode or scale with intentional harmonic behavior;
- reproducible capture of a Seed, parameters, orbit summary, and audio settings;
- a stable share format without silently exposing private data;
- user-facing feedback for invalid, singular, paused, and incomplete states.

## 下一阶段——音乐与交互研究

先选择并记录音乐规则，再开始实现：

- 将旋律、低音、节奏和受控变化作为明确的音乐层；
- 使用共享调式或音阶，并明确和声行为；
- 可复现地捕获 Seed、参数、轨迹摘要和音频设置；
- 建立不悄悄暴露隐私数据的稳定分享格式；
- 为无效、奇异、暂停和不完整状态提供明确反馈。

## Later — Productization

- Multiple Seeds only when the musical model benefits from them.
- Additional mathematical studies with independent references and tests.
- Public sharing, performance telemetry, and content operations only after privacy, accessibility, performance, and recovery gates are defined.
- A formal release channel with changelog, saved Sites version, deployment record, and rollback target.

## 后续——产品化

- 只有在音乐模型确实受益时才引入多 Seed。
- 为新的数学研究建立独立资料依据与测试。
- 隐私、无障碍、性能和恢复关卡明确后，再做公开分享、演奏数据与内容运营。
- 建立带有变更记录、Sites 保存版本、部署记录和回滚目标的正式发布通道。

## Not Yet Claimed

This roadmap does not claim strict mathematical theorems, unlimited orbit behavior, real-device Safari acceptance, production-scale performance, or a finished multi-voice composition system.

这份路线图不宣称严格数学定理、无限轨道行为、真实设备 Safari 验收、生产规模性能或完成的多声部作曲系统。
