# Formody — Harmonic Playground

**拨形见声 · An interactive playground where mathematics, art, and sound meet.**

Formody is an experimental space for turning mathematical relationships into interactive forms, visual rhythms, and sonic responses. It is made for touching, tuning, observing, and discovering—not just looking at a finished image.

## Why Formody

Formody explores the moment when a rule becomes a gesture, a pattern becomes a feeling, and sound becomes something you can play with.

- **Mathematical thinking** as structure, rhythm, proportion, and transformation
- **Artistic expression** as form, atmosphere, composition, and surprise
- **Sound** as response, movement, resonance, and memory
- **Interaction** as the bridge between an idea and a lived experience

## Experience

The project is designed as a space for active exploration:

1. Change a parameter or gesture.
2. Watch the form respond.
3. Listen for the emerging resonance.
4. Find a relationship that feels unexpectedly alive.

## Status

Formody is currently an evolving experience prototype. The interface, interaction model, visual language, and sound system will continue to grow through making and testing.
